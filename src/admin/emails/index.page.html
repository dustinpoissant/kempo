Emails

Kempo does not send email itself: an email extension (such as kempo-resend or kempo-smtp) does. How long the log is kept, and how many times a failed email is tried again, are on the System tab of Settings.

Every email the site was asked to send, and what happened to it.

The emails this site sends: kempo's own and any the site has added. Preview draws one the way it would be sent, with made-up values when the template has a .sample.json beside it. Edit one to reword it; your copy is used from the next email on and Reset to default brings the packaged one back.
